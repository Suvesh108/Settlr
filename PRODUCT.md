## Platform

## Product Purpose

Settlr is a zero-friction, local-first group and solitary expense tracker. It provides automatic debt netting, mathematical debt simplification (zero-sum minimum cash flow transfers), and automated bank/UPI debit detection via SMS and notifications without requiring passwords, accounts, or cloud surveillance.

## Users

1. **Shared Living & Trips**: Roommates, flatmates, travel groups who share expenses and need transparent, automated debt settlement without manually recalculating who owes whom.
2. **Solitary Spenders**: Individual budgeters who want private, frictionless logging of their personal daily spends alongside their group commitments.

## Positioning

- **Zero-Friction Zero-Logins**: Local-first identity switching with cryptographic invite codes. No mandatory passwords or phone verification.
- **Automated Debit Capture**: AirPods-style dynamic payment allocation card triggers instantly on bank SMS debits, letting users allocate costs in 1 tap to "Group" or "Personal".
- **Strict Debt Netting**: Automatic bipartite graph resolution for minimum cash-flow bilateral settlement.

## Brand & Voice

- **Name**: Settlr
- **Design Ethos**: SaasInterface-inspired, clean white architecture, subtle borders, high visual density, haptic micro-interactions, mobile-dock first.
- **Palette**: Clean white, neutral-50/100/200, sky-500 accent for active transactions, emerald-500 for settled states, rose-500 for debts.
